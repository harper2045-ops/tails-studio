Privacy Policy: Tails Of History Studio Tools

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Tails Of History Studio Tools ("the tool") is an internal application operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Tails Of History Official. It is used only by that owner, on their own computer, with their own Google account.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the tool accesses

  • Basic information about the signed-in owner's own YouTube channel (channel name and ID).
  • The owner's own videos and their metadata, for uploading and scheduling.
  • Aggregate YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's own channel (for example views, watch time, likes).

The tool does not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube user or viewer.

How data is used, stored and shared

  • Data is used only to upload the owner's videos and to show the owner their own channel statistics.
  • OAuth access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in files readable only by the owner. Analytics results are shown on screen and may be kept in local working notes on that computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or sent to any server other than Google's APIs.

Cookies and device information

The tool does not place cookies or collect device information. It is a command-line program, not a website or app used by the public.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings. Deleting the tool's local token files removes all stored credentials. Stored data is deleted or refreshed at least every 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.
